Your routine stays on your Mac

Flowly is a native macOS application by Studio 418. It stores timer state, preferences, hydration entries and break/focus history locally on your Mac using SwiftData. Flowly does not send these records to Studio 418 or operate a backend, account system, cloud sync, advertising or analytics service.

Notifications and permissions

With your permission, macOS delivers local reminders. No push notification server is used. You can disable reminders in Flowly and change notification permissions in macOS System Settings.

Retention and deletion

Records remain on your Mac until you delete them or remove the app’s local data. To clear Flowly’s timer, preferences and activity, open Settings → About → Delete local data and confirm. This also turns reminders off. Copies in backups you manage are governed by your backup settings.

Support and this website

If you choose to email support, we receive the address and information you include and use it to respond. Do not include sensitive health information. You can request deletion of support correspondence at studio418apps@gmail.com; information required for legal obligations may need to be retained. Email providers process correspondence under their own terms.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics or advertising cookies. GitHub may process technical visitor information such as IP addresses to provide and secure hosting. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Apple handles App Store and operating-system services under its own privacy policies.

Contact and changes

Questions or privacy requests: studio418apps@gmail.com. We will update this page if Flowly’s practices change.

Rutinin Mac’inde kalır

Flowly, Studio 418 tarafından geliştirilen yerel bir macOS uygulamasıdır. Sayaç durumu, tercihler, su kayıtları ve mola/odaklanma geçmişi SwiftData ile Mac’inde saklanır. Flowly bu kayıtları Studio 418’e göndermez; sunucu, hesap sistemi, bulut eşzamanlama, reklam veya analiz hizmeti kullanmaz.

Bildirimler ve izinler

İzin verirsen macOS yerel hatırlatmaları gösterir. Anlık bildirim sunucusu kullanılmaz. Hatırlatmaları Flowly’den kapatabilir, bildirim iznini macOS Sistem Ayarları’ndan değiştirebilirsin.

Saklama ve silme

Kayıtlar, sen silene veya uygulamanın yerel verilerini kaldırana kadar Mac’inde kalır. Sayacı, tercihleri ve etkinlikleri silmek için Ayarlar → Hakkında → Yerel verileri sil yolunu izle ve onayla. Bu işlem hatırlatmaları da kapatır. Yönettiğin yedeklerdeki kopyalar yedekleme ayarlarına bağlıdır.
